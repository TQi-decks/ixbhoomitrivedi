01
Why this month

Navratri content has a very short shelf life

A garba ground is the rare place where the entire audience is holding a phone, in the mood, and posting. The night is the moment. Three days later it is an archive.

This is the one month of the year when Bhoomi's audience actively goes looking for her. People at the ground film her from the floor, people who could not get in search for her on Instagram, and the diaspora watches from a different time zone. Every one of those people is already on the page. The only question is what is waiting for them when they arrive.

Our answer is simple. Somebody who understands the page should be in the room every night, cutting what just happened and putting it up before the night ends. Everything around those ten nights, the build up before and the recap after, is built to make the ten nights land harder.

05
How it works in practice

The same night turnaround

Publishing on the night only works if the approval loop is designed for it in advance. This is the part that decides whether the whole idea holds.

01

Capture

On ground from soundcheck. Footage offloaded in blocks through the night.

02

Cut

Edited on site to a pre agreed format so there is no blank page at midnight.

03

Clear

Sent to one named approver on WhatsApp. Thirty minute window, then it goes.

04

Publish

Live on the page the same night, with caption, tags and collaborator invites in place.

Two things we would like agreed before night one. First, a single named approver with a thirty minute response window, and a small set of formats pre approved so the routine cuts do not need approval at all. Second, written confirmation of what we may film and publish from the ground, and the sponsor and branding visibility you want held in frame. Both take one conversation now and save ten arguments in October.

One practical note we handle rather than hand over: live audio of film tracks is regularly flagged on Reels and can get a post muted after it is live. We build around it, using performance audio, crowd sound and format choices that keep the reel intact, and we monitor the posts after they go up.

06
Phase three · 21 to 27 October

Afterglow

Ten nights of footage is far more content than ten nights of posting can carry.

By the 21st we are sitting on the best raw material of Bhoomi's year, and the dandiya calendar is still running. This week is where the archive earns its keep: the recap edit of the season, the moments that were too good to rush out on the night, crowd reaction cuts, song specific pieces and the pieces that tee up whatever comes next.

It also means the account does not fall off a cliff on the 21st, which is what usually happens to artist pages the week after a festival.

08
How we plan to use the ten nights

Formats we would run

Ten nights of the same wide stage shot is ten nights of the same post. These are the formats we would build the season around, locked with your team at kickoff.

a
A recurring nightly formatOne format that runs all ten nights so the season becomes a series people follow, rather than ten unrelated uploads. Night one teaches the audience what to come back for.
b
The colour of the dayNavratri has a colour for each night and the whole ground dresses for it. Building one of the two daily pieces around the day's colour gives us a built in visual system and a reason for people to check the page before they leave the house. We lock the final colour order against the panchang at kickoff.
c
Collaborator posts across both handlesPublishing the night's reel as a collaboration between Bhoomi's page and the Rang Raas handle puts the same post in front of two audiences at once. It costs nothing, it drives ticket intent while the season is still running, and since both handles are inside this conversation it is simple to set up.
d
The crowd, not just the stageThe thing people share is themselves. Floor level footage of the ground going up during a song consistently outruns a clean stage shot.
e
The hour beforeGetting ready, soundcheck, the walk to stage. Low effort to capture when you are already there all night, and it is the material that makes an audience feel close to an artist.
f
Song led cutsSpecific moments from the songs the audience came for, cut so they work as standalone pieces and stay useful long after October.

Access, voice and control

How we would work on the account

Same night publishing and comment management both assume we can act on the account directly. We would rather set that up properly than work around it.

a
The account in scopeThis engagement covers Bhoomi Trivedi's official Instagram handle. The Rang Raas handle is carried through collaborator posts on the live nights, and can be brought into full scope as an addition if you would like it managed alongside.
b
Posting access, or delivery insteadOur preference is direct access to the handle, because that is what makes a same night turnaround possible at all. If the artist's team would rather hold access, we deliver every asset with its caption and tags into a shared folder on a fixed schedule and they publish. The only trade off is speed on the live nights, and we would plan the formats around that from the start.
c
Writing as BhoomiBefore anything goes out we agree a short voice note: how she speaks, what she does and does not comment on, which words are hers and which are not. Nothing gets improvised in her voice.
d
An escalation lineAnything personal, press related, sensitive or unusual is not answered by us. It goes straight to you, same day, with a note on what we saw.

Beyond October

What you own at the end of the month

Ten nights of professionally captured footage of a full house is not a byproduct of this engagement. It is one of the main things you are buying.

Most Navratri events come out of the season with a folder of stage photographs and whatever the crowd happened to post. You would come out of it with a logged, backed up archive of all ten nights, shot deliberately, already cut into pieces that work.

That archive keeps paying long after October. It is the recap that closes the season on social. It is the proof of scale that goes into next year's sponsor conversations, showing a full ground rather than describing one. It is the material that opens ticket sales for Rang Raas 2027. And it is footage of Bhoomi at her best, which serves the artist side of this just as well.
